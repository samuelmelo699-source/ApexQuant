📰 Daily recap

📈 Prop analyzer · NBA

Consensus line from the sportsbooks, the model's projection and cover %, this season's over/under record, and a game-by-game chart with a line slider for alt lines. Hit % uses the last 10 games, or the whole season until a player has 10.

🥇 First basket · NBA

Every book's first-scorer board from your Odds API key. Each book's prices are de-vigged across the whole field (power method, which strips the extra margin books put on long shots). The best price for each player is then compared with the other books' consensus. Ranked by expected value.

🏀 NBA first basket

Rules to know: most books settle on the first field goal, so free throws don't count. Check your book. A player who doesn't play is usually voided. "Model" = 75% data + 25% projection. Data: the chance his team wins the tip and scores first (opening jump balls in recent games) × how often he takes his team's first shot among tonight's five starters (recent play-by-play). Projection: his share of the starters' projected scoring.

📒 Bet tracker & closing-line value

Closing-line value (CLV) is your price compared with the fair price at kick-off. Beating the close on average is the clearest proof of an edge, long before profit settles down. Prop closes are captured automatically in the last 15 minutes before start while the app is open. Use "Capture close" to do it by hand.

0.25 = quarter-Kelly (recommended)

📐 Model report card

Is the model actually right? Before each game the app records what it predicted for every market with a real book price. After the game, ESPN's final box score grades each one. Compare the model with the market here, and see which markets to trust.

Games are graded automatically about 3 hours after they start, while the app is open.
